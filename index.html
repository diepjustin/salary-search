Methodology & Notes

Salary is the annual amount budgeted for the position at the listed FTE, not take-home pay. It excludes benefits, and for coaches it excludes outside income and incentive clauses. A part-time appointment (FTE below 1.000) is paid that fraction of a full-time salary. Rows named “TBD” or “TBF” are budget lines with no employee assigned.
